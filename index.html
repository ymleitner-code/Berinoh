Two ways to fund our community's weddings

The committee is weighing two designs for a community wedding fund. They help families in opposite ways: one gives the money on the day but the amount grows over the years, the other gives the full amount but after a wait. Watch the narrated presentation, open either simulator to test it, or compare them side by side below.

Watch the narrated presentation

Model A

Gemach Hakehiloh

A family saves together and pays a small monthly fee. When a child marries, the fund lends what it can afford that year, on the wedding date, and the loan grows toward the full amount as the fund matures. It sits in a credit-union framework and grows with the community.

Open the Gemach Hakehiloh simulator
Model B

Gemach Hamerkazi

A family saves for each child and joins a waiting list. When its turn comes it receives the full interest-free loan, and its repayments help the next family in line. It runs on a steady flow of joiners and, once mature, largely funds itself.
